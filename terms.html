Terms & acceptable use

Use AliasTrace for legitimate public-footprint research—not harassment with a prettier dashboard.

1. Service

AliasTrace checks fixed public profile and API locations for aliases supplied by a user. Results may include confirmed, uncertain, unavailable or blocked checks. It does not establish identity, ownership, intent or wrongdoing.

2. Permitted use

You may use the service for your own footprint, security research with authorization, brand or account monitoring, journalism and other lawful research that respects the rights and safety of others.

3. Prohibited use

  • Harassment, stalking, doxxing, intimidation or discrimination.
  • Targeting minors or vulnerable people without a legitimate safeguarding purpose.
  • Credential attacks, account takeover, impersonation or bypassing access controls.
  • Bulk surveillance, reselling personal profiles, automated high-volume access or evading rate limits.
  • Using results as proof that accounts belong to one person without independent verification.
  • Any illegal act or violation of a platform’s applicable terms.

4. Accuracy

Aliases are not unique identifiers. Platforms change, block requests, reuse names and return misleading status pages with impressive enthusiasm. Verify important findings at the linked source. Results are provided without a guarantee of completeness or accuracy.

5. Availability and limits

Scans may be slowed, refused or stopped to protect platforms and the service. The three-alias limit, destination allowlist, concurrency controls and cooldowns must not be circumvented. Access may be suspended for abuse.

6. Third-party services

Platform names and marks belong to their respective owners. AliasTrace is not affiliated with or endorsed by them. Linked services are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

7. Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, the service is provided “as is.” The operator is not liable for decisions made from unverified results, third-party availability, lost reports or indirect damages. Nothing excludes liability that cannot legally be excluded.
