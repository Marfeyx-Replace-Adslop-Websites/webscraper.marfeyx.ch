Privacy policy

Marfeyx' Webscraper is designed for public-footprint self-audits with short retention and no advertising profiles.

1. Controller and contact

Marfeyx' Webscraper is operated by Marfeyx in Switzerland. Privacy questions, access requests, objections and deletion requests can be sent to dev@marfeyx.ch.

2. Data processed

When you start a scan, the service processes the aliases you submit, a random browser identifier, a one-way hash derived from request metadata for abuse prevention, requested platform identifiers, public profile responses, scan timing and technical error information. If you request email delivery, the recipient email address is processed for that delivery.

Marfeyx' Webscraper does not ask for platform passwords, private profiles, contact lists, precise location, payment details or government identifiers.

3. Sources and purpose

Profile information comes from publicly accessible profile pages and public platform APIs. It is processed only to produce the report requested by the user, distinguish confirmed matches from uncertain checks, secure the service and enforce rate limits. A matching alias does not prove that profiles belong to the same person.

4. Legal basis

Processing is based on the user’s request to provide the service and the operator’s legitimate interest in protecting it from abuse. Email delivery is based on the recipient’s explicit request. Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection requirements apply; GDPR rights may also apply where its territorial scope is met.

5. Retention

Scan records and results are scheduled for automatic deletion within 24 hours. Rate-limit hashes may be retained for up to 48 hours. Email addresses are deleted after delivery or a failed delivery attempt, and are not added to a marketing list. Reports downloaded to a device remain under that device owner’s control.

6. Recipients and international processing

GitHub Pages serves the static website. Supabase hosts the database and Edge Function used for controlled platform checks. The selected Supabase region should be in Europe. Public requests necessarily go to each platform selected by the catalogue and may therefore be processed in other countries under that platform’s policy. If transactional email is enabled, the configured email delivery provider processes the recipient address and report content solely for delivery.

7. Local storage and cookies

The site stores a random abuse-prevention client identifier and, while a scan is active, its short-lived reconnect capability in browser storage. This lets the interface reconnect after Safari is closed. It does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Platforms opened from report links apply their own policies.

8. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request information, access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to processing. Contact dev@marfeyx.ch and include the scan identifier if available. You may also contact the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner or an applicable EU/EEA supervisory authority.

9. Security and limits

Marfeyx' Webscraper uses fixed destinations, low concurrency, strict input validation, short timeouts, origin checks, rate limiting and database access controls. No internet service can promise absolute security. Do not submit aliases when you lack a lawful and legitimate reason to research them.

10. Changes

Material changes will be reflected by the date at the top of this page. The current version applies whenever you start a new scan.
